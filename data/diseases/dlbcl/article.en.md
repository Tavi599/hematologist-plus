# Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma

**ICD-10: C83.3.** An aggressive B-cell lymphoma; the most common non-Hodgkin lymphoma in adults.

## Diagnosis

- Histology of an excised lymph node or involved tissue with immunohistochemistry. A needle aspirate alone is not sufficient.
- Immunophenotype: CD20+, CD79a+; CD10, BCL6, MUM1, BCL2, MYC and Ki-67 are also assessed.
- FISH for MYC, BCL2 and BCL6 rearrangements to identify double-hit disease.

## Work-up before treatment

- Ann Arbor staging: CT of neck, chest, abdomen and pelvis, or PET-CT.
- Bone marrow trephine when PET-CT is not available or cytopenias are present.
- Full blood count, biochemistry, LDH, urate, creatinine, liver function, β2 microglobulin.
- Hepatitis B and C, HIV. Antiviral prophylaxis when HBcAb positive.
- ECG and echocardiography before anthracyclines.
- IPI score and ECOG performance status.
